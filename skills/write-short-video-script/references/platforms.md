# Platforms: limits, signals and rules

Consulted on 2026-10-26 (Instagram for Creators, Adam Mosseri, TikTok Community Guidelines and TikTok for Business, YouTube Help and blog, LinkedIn Help and guides); the links in "Sources" were checked on 2026-11-20. A statement in the table that has no link there is given as reported. These facts change: before stating one in public, check it against the platform's current help pages.

## Safe zone for on-screen text

On the 1080x1920 canvas, essential content goes between y 210 and y 1680, with a 60 px margin on each side (x 60 to 1020). Nothing essential in the button column on the right (x 945 to 1080 between y 1095 and 1850), and no subtitle or other text that must be read while the video plays below y 1400, which is how high the viewer's caption block rises. These are the figures of `edit-short-video/references/visual-system.md`, which explains how they were measured and is the source to follow if it differs.

## Implications for the script

1. **The hook is designed for 3 seconds in silence** on all four platforms; on TikTok, the proposition must be clear at 3 and consolidated at 6.
2. **Write for watch time and sends**, not for comments. A "sendable" moment (an idea someone wants to DM to a colleague) is worth more than a "comment X".
3. **Length by intent, within 3 minutes.** Every additional 15 seconds must bring new information.
4. **Text inside the safe zone** described above.
5. **Original material.** If the piece reacts to someone else's content, it must add context, information or a new point of view.
6. **AI in the process is not labeled; AI that simulates reality is.** An avatar or a generated scene that looks real requires a label.
7. **LinkedIn requires adaptation**: professional but human tone, clean audio and image, 15 seconds to 2 minutes, business value or learning as the reason.
8. **Test hooks with Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers first, metrics about 24 hours after sharing) when in doubt between two openings.
9. **Evaluate with rates, not totals**: average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach on Instagram; "stayed to watch", engaged views and average duration on YouTube. Raw views do not measure retention on either.
