description: Get the background music and sound effects (SFX) for a creator's short video from sources with a known license. Translates the piece's needs into a sound brief, picks from the semantic sound catalog (what plays when a text enters, when there is money, an error, a success, a transition, a comic punchline), reuses the workspace's own pool in `media/audio/`, searches Freesound by API (CC0 by default, CC BY with credit), builds the brand motif sounds with its generators, verifies every file with ffmpeg (duration, loudness, loop join) and records the license and attribution in the piece record. # Find Video Audio

Sound is half of the premium finish and the part that most quickly gives away a cheap edit: a whoosh on every cut, music that competes with the voice, or an effect that does not match the energy of the movement. This skill chooses little and well: every sound is tied to a specific movement, comes from a source with a known license or from the workspace's own pool, and is recorded so it is not repeated piece after piece.

Write everything the user reads (chat, questions, the texts of `sounds.json`, the choice page, the piece record and the proposal) in the user's language, or the one the workspace instructions set. The choice page takes its own interface texts from `labels.json` (step 6).

The style and source rules below are defaults; the brand guide and the workspace instructions win when they say otherwise. Recording the license and origin of every file is not a default: it always applies.

## Load context

Read `references/sound-catalog.md` (situations and the sound that matches each one, plus the default library folders) and `references/sources-and-licenses.md` (what each source allows, how it is queried and the columns of the two register files). Read `references/music-brief.md` when the piece has music. Read `media/audio/inventory.md` (every library file, folder by folder, with duration, origin, license and notes) and `media/audio/log.md` (what was prepared and used, piece by piece). Read the piece's edit proposal (`social/<piece>/edit/proposal.md`) if it exists; when `edit-short-video` invokes this skill, it arrives with the cut and the layer map already written and the sound section empty: this skill completes it. Read the "Sound identity" section of `brand-guide/motion.md` if the brand guide has one (a motif, fixed system sounds, how much sound the edit carries).

Default sources: free ones with a known license. A paid library is used only when the user has a license for it and says so; its files then enter the library like any other, with that license in their inventory row. Default style, unless `brand-guide/motion.md` says otherwise: zooms and camera changes carry sound, with more presence than a sober edit but never one sound per sentence. The agent cannot hear: it proposes candidates with measurements, and the user decides by listening. That is why the library in `media/audio/` holds only what the user chose by ear, and why a selection made only by name and duration is never final.

## Flow

1. **Understand the piece.** Format (the workspace's own formats in `editorial/`; common ones are educational, demo, opinion, progress log and sketch), duration, tone, moments of the layer map that call for sound (text entrances, punch-ins, zooms, block transitions, emphasis, figures, errors, successes, punchlines) and whether it has music. If there is no edit proposal, ask the caller for the list of moments with times.
2. **Sound brief.** A table: moment, time, catalog sound type, character (low or high, short or long, dry or with tail), level relative to the voice. For music: mood, energy, approximate BPM, instrumentation, duration, change points, with or without a dry ending. Template in `references/music-brief.md`.
3. **Search in this order** and stop as soon as there are enough candidates:
   1. The workspace's own pool `media/audio/` (by default, avoid repeating a music track used in the last 8 pieces, which `log.md` shows; system SFX do repeat because they are the sound signature). SFX live in `media/audio/sfx/<type>/`, music in `media/audio/music/<mood>/` (default folders in "Library folders" of `references/sound-catalog.md`), and `media/audio/inventory.md` lists every file with its duration, origin, license and notes; keep it up to date when a file comes in or goes out. The pool is the user's selection and takes precedence over any external search; "Where to look in the library" in `references/sound-catalog.md` says which folder to check for each situation. A library file with no row in `log.md` has not been prepared yet: before using it, normalize it with `prepare-sfx.sh` and verify it with `audio-check.sh`; its log row (step 7) records that preparation.
   2. Freesound by API with `scripts/freesound-search.sh`: CC0 by default, CC BY (`--license by` or `any`) when the user accepts crediting the author in the caption. The script never lists or downloads a sound under a non-commercial or any other license. Skipped without `FREESOUND_API_KEY`.
   3. Catalogs without API. Pixabay and Mixkit (music and SFX) and Bensound (music): propose specific searches for the user to download by hand, saving the license receipt. The terms of Pixabay and Mixkit forbid automated downloading and those of Bensound are not verified, so the agent does not download from them, with a script or with a browser. Incompetech (music, CC BY 4.0) is the exception: it publishes its catalog and the folder of its MP3 files for automated agents, so the agent downloads the chosen track itself, one file at a time, and saves the receipt ("Without API" in `references/sources-and-licenses.md`). Without a Freesound key, this is the route for an SFX with a clean license.
   4. If nothing fits, say so and deliver the brief of the missing sound as a pending item. This skill generates only the brand motif sounds ("Brand motif" in `references/sound-catalog.md`); it operates no AI music or SFX model. `references/sources-and-licenses.md` lists the local models the user can install on their own and what their licenses allow.

   myinstants is not part of this order: it is off by default, because it declares no license for its sounds ("Sources outside the default" in `references/sources-and-licenses.md`). The agent does not search or download from it: if the user wants a sound from there, they download it themselves and the agent verifies and records it.
4. **Verify** every candidate with `scripts/audio-check.sh`: duration, sample peak, mean level, leading and trailing silence, broken files and whether the levels of the start and the end join for a loop. Discard what does not match the energy of the movement (a bright whoosh on a slow entrance feels wrong).
   - Duplicates: before anything enters the library, `python3 scripts/audio-duplicates.py <new files folder> media/audio/sfx media/audio/music`. The same sound arrives under another name or re-encoded, and comparing bytes or names does not catch it.
   - Looping music: find where the track's level starts falling at the end (many have a fade to silence of several seconds) and where the intro reaches the level of the body, with the level curve command in "Loop points" of `references/music-brief.md`. The bed is built with those two times ("Music bed" recipe in `edit-short-video/references/hyperframes-recipes.md`), and the user hears the join on the choice page: a level match does not prove a musical one.
5. **Prepare** the candidates in `social/<piece>/edit/candidates/sfx/` and `social/<piece>/edit/candidates/music/`, never in a temporary folder. SFX with `scripts/prepare-sfx.sh` (trims edge silence and normalizes the sample peak to -3 dBFS in 48 kHz WAV); music with `loudnorm` to -34 LUFS integrated as a starting point (20 dB under a voice at -14), as 256 kbps MP3 or WAV, with the command in `references/music-brief.md`. The final level relative to the voice is set in the mix, not in the file. For a multi-hit sound synced to what is on screen (keys, tiles, clicks), `scripts/hit-times.sh <audio>` writes `<audio name>.hits.json` next to it with the instant of each hit in seconds; the animation uses those instants.
6. **Listening and approval.** The agent preselects by measurements and by name, and the user decides by listening. No sound enters the project without this step, and approving the edit proposal does not approve the sounds.
   1. Write `social/<piece>/edit/candidates/sounds.json`, a list with one row per moment of the layer map and one row for the music bed if there are candidates: `clip` (row id, a string such as `"03"`), `time`, `moment`, `on_screen` (what happens on screen), `spoken` (what the creator says at that instant), `purpose` (what the sound has to convey), the agent's `favorite` with its `reason`, and one to three `options` (`file`, a path relative to the candidates folder such as `sfx/<file>` or `music/<file>`, and `note` with its origin). Signature sounds go with `"signature": true` and a single option, to be heard in context.
   2. If the user's language is not English, write `social/<piece>/edit/candidates/labels.json` with the page's interface texts translated; its keys are the `LABELS` block at the top of `scripts/choose-sounds.py`.
   3. Generate the page: `python3 scripts/choose-sounds.py social/<piece>/edit/candidates "<title>"` (template in `assets/choose-sounds.html`). It stops with a message if a row lacks what is seen, what is said or what the sound is for, if `clip` is not a string or if an option's file does not exist.
   4. Serve it as a background task with `python3 <skills>/analyze-video/scripts/serve-choice-page.py social/<piece>/edit/candidates 8767 sounds.json choose-sounds.html`, check that it answers (`curl -sf -o /dev/null http://localhost:8767/choose-sounds.html`) and give the user the local link: every selection is saved automatically as `"chosen"` in `sounds.json`.
   5. When the user says they are done: read `sounds.json` (a row without `"chosen"` keeps the favorite), stop the background task that serves the page and note the choice in the Sound section of the proposal.
7. **Move to the library and to the project.** What is reusable goes to `media/audio/sfx/<type>/` or `media/audio/music/<mood>/` (existing folders; a new one only if no type fits) and gets its row in `media/audio/inventory.md`; the prepared version of a library file replaces the original in its folder. What was chosen is copied to `social/<piece>/edit/project/assets/sfx/` and `social/<piece>/edit/project/assets/music/`, each multi-hit sound with its `.hits.json`. Add one row per file to `media/audio/log.md` (how it was prepared and where the piece uses it), and write the Audio section of `piece.md` with the files used and the attribution the license requires. The columns of both register files are in "Register" of `references/sources-and-licenses.md`.
8. **Deliver** to the caller: a table of moments with the chosen file for each, the music with its license, its loop times and the attribution text, and the pending items (manual downloads, sounds no source had). The final level relative to the voice is validated in the review render.

Ask the user with the structured question tool only when the choice changes the piece: music yes or no in an ambiguous piece, using a source without a declared license, accepting a CC BY credit in the caption, or choosing between two music beds. Always present the candidates for listening before considering anything chosen.

## Rules

- No in-app library (they do not work with burned-in audio) and no audio that belongs to someone else: commercial music, dialogue from films, series or games, or the recognizable sound of a product or a platform, even when it circulates in sound catalogs. Describe the sound that is needed in generic terms (a cash register, an error beep) and find it under a known license.
- One sound per movement; if a movement is both visual and semantic (a screenshot showing an error comes in), the semantic sound wins and the whoosh is dropped. Never two identical sounds in a row, except the ticks and pops of a list being built, which repeat by design. Never an SFX that covers a syllable: a cut hit between takes attacks on the frame of the cut and its tail ends before the first syllable of the new sentence (`edit-short-video/scripts/verify-sfx.py` measures it). Exits sound shorter and quieter than entrances.
- If the layer map asks for more sounds than the piece can take (more than one every 3 seconds on average), silence in this order: pills and pinned terms, element exits, list items from the fourth on, shot cuts between similar sizes. Emphasis zooms, block transitions and semantic sounds stay.
- System SFX (dry cut hit, entrance whoosh, emphasis hit, list tick) are fixed once in the pool and repeat in every piece: they are the brand's sound signature. What changes per piece are the semantic sounds (money, error, success) and the music.
- A sound brought by the user is respected as is: only what they asked for is changed. If they ask to adjust the timing, each hit is moved to its instant without touching its volume, its tail or its timbre, without "humanizing" with volume variations and without normalizing (the level relative to the voice is set in the mix). If an extra adjustment seems useful, propose it separately so they can listen and decide.
- Always record license and origin. With CC BY, the attribution goes in the caption in the format "Music: <title> by <author> (CC BY 4.0)", written in the caption's language. A file from a source without a declared license is recorded as "origin without declared license" with its link, and is not presented as licensed.
- This skill publishes nothing and installs no software.
