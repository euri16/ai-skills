# Baking soda vs baking powder: the pancake test

- Status: approved
- Time zone: America/Chicago
- Type: experiment
- Pillar: Ingredient roles and swaps
- Central idea: Baking soda needs an acid in the batter and baking powder brings its own, so swapping one for the other without checking for an acid changes the rise, the color and the taste.
- Hook: C4 X versus Y: "Same batter, same pan. So why is this one flat?"
