# Review and approval

The checks a Crumb Theory piece passes before it goes out, and what needs the explicit approval of the user. Read by `write-short-video-script` and applied by every skill that proposes, renders, schedules or publishes.

## Check 1: The question

- The hook asks a question the video actually answers.
- The answer arrives before the viewer has a reason to leave, with some proof in the first 10 seconds.
- The piece fits a pillar in `../editorial/pillars.md` and serves the audience in `../brand-guide/audiences.md`.

## Check 2: The science

- Each mechanism and number is in the claims ledger with a source from `research-policy.md`.
- The test followed the kitchen test protocol, and the script says how it was done.
- The claim stays inside what the test showed; the limit is stated when leaving it out would mislead.
- Simplifications are marked as such.
- Units and temperatures are correct in both systems.

## Check 3: The kitchen

- Every result on screen is the real result of the test.
- Comparisons are labeled "Test" and "Control" and framed the same way.
- Nothing staged or generated is presented as a bake.
- Generated images, if any, are labeled and approved.

## Check 4: The voice

- Sounds curious, warm and dry, as `../brand-guide/voice-and-humor.md` describes.
- Humor is aimed at the bake or the oven, never at a person.
- No banned words ("foolproof", "hack", "secret"), no health claims.
- No invented anecdote, reaction or disaster.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere.

## Check 5: The channel

- Length, structure and CTA as in the channel guide (`../editorial/instagram.md`).
- Subtitles present, legible on a phone and outside the interface zones.
- Cover approved and checked in the profile grid.
- Caption approved per channel; YouTube title under 100 characters.
- Music and effects licensed, with any required credit in the caption.
- Visual review checklist of `../brand-guide/visual-identity.md` passed at full size.

## What needs explicit approval

The user approves each of these before the next step happens:

| Step | What is shown for approval |
|---|---|
| Plan of a script | Angle, payoff, hook options and beats |
| Script | The full script, with its word count and pending verifications |
| Edit | The edit proposal, before any final render |
| Sounds | The music and effects, listened to before they are used |
| Cover | The cover at full size and in the profile grid |
| Caption | The exact text for each channel |
| Scheduling or publishing | Channel, date, time with its time zone, file, caption and title |

Nothing is published, scheduled, sent, deleted or committed to publicly without that explicit yes. The approval request always shows the full final text or asset, any sensitive claim, what is still pending and the exact action that will happen.

## Corrections

If a published piece turns out to be wrong, say so publicly: pin a comment with the correction, update the caption, and record the correction and its date in the piece's `piece.md`. Never fix a claim silently.
