# Content pillars

The five pillars every Crumb Theory piece is classified under. Read by `research-content-topic` and `write-short-video-script` to place an idea, and used in the Pillar column of `social/index.md` to keep the mix balanced.

## 1. Why it failed

Diagnosis from symptoms: dense bread, flat cookies, a sunken cake, tunnels in muffins, a gummy layer at the bottom. The piece starts from what the viewer saw and ends with the most likely cause and one fix.

Guiding questions:

- What does the failure look like, exactly?
- Which single cause explains most of it?
- What else could cause the same symptom, and how do you tell them apart?
- What is the one change for the next bake?

## 2. Ingredient roles and swaps

What each ingredient does in the bowl and what changes when you replace it: baking soda versus baking powder, butter versus oil, brown versus white sugar, bread flour versus all-purpose, eggs and their stand-ins.

Always say what the swap changes besides the obvious (taste, color, spread, shelf life) and what to adjust to compensate.

## 3. Myth or method

Common baking advice put to the test: room-temperature eggs, sifting flour, never opening the oven door, resting cookie dough. Each one ends with a verdict and its condition: holds up, holds up only when, or does not make a difference you can see in a home kitchen.

A verdict rests on the test and on the sources in `../operations/research-policy.md`, never on one trial alone.

## 4. Kitchen experiments

Side-by-side tests with one variable, run on camera: the same batter baked at two temperatures, two flours in the same recipe, kneaded versus no-knead. The method is shown so the viewer can repeat it, and the limits of a home test are stated.

## 5. Heat and time

Ovens and thermometers, preheating, hot spots, carryover baking, cooling, and why recipes say "or until". Practical, because most failures that are not about ingredients are about heat.

## Outside the pillars

- **Nutrition and health:** not covered.
- **Food safety:** only to point to official guidance when a piece touches raw flour, raw eggs, storage or allergens.
- **Recipes on their own:** only when a recipe is the setting for one of the pillars.
- **Equipment:** only when the tool changes the result, and never as a review.

A piece that fits none of the pillars is recorded with `none` in the index and needs a reason to exist.
